# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Shicheng Fan, Kun Zhang, and Lu Cheng for developing TRACE,
formalizing continuous mechanism evolution, releasing the implementation, and
making the synthetic and real-data protocol surfaces available for independent
scrutiny. The public source and explicit trajectory experiments make it
possible to separate theorem attribution, finite risk bounds, learned
interpolation, comparator availability, and metric limitations.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit. It does not imply review,
approval, endorsement, or collaboration by the paper’s authors.
