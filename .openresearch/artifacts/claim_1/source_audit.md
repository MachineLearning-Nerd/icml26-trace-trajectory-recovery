# Claim 1 source audit

Pinned paper: arXiv `2601.21135v2`; ar5iv source SHA-256
`24c2ba7f5468f0a9f9ae414c8de764821e8c0655ec5b1c72807cf6834096427d`.

The title of Theorem 4.1 is “Identifiability of Latent Variables.” Its
conclusion is only that learned latent coordinates are component-wise strictly
monotonic transforms of permuted true coordinates under sufficient
variability.

The first sentence of Section 4.2 explicitly says Theorem 4.1 does not address
inference of the mixing trajectory. Pointwise trajectory recovery is Theorem
4.2 and smooth trajectory recovery is Theorem 4.3. Therefore, the imported
Claim 1 attribution to Theorem 4.1 alone is contradicted by the primary source.

This is a narrow source-level falsification. The broader statement that the
paper presents latent and trajectory results across Theorems 4.1–4.3 is not
contradicted.
